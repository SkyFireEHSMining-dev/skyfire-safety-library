# SkyFire Quarterly Release Communications Policy

**Product-owner decision:** November 17, 2026 (Pacific)  
**Status:** Standing communications and release-documentation instruction  
**Scope:** SkyFire Safety Library releases; does not authorize a new development sprint

## Purpose
Publish SkyFire updates on **LinkedIn no more than once per calendar quarter** as a sustainable, voluntary cadence. Posting is optional when there is nothing substantive to share. The product owner may choose to share release notes alone, a brief combined quarterly release summary, or a different relevant SkyFire update.

## Required actions at every release closeout
1. Preserve the **full final versioned release notes** in GitHub under `docs/` and as a dated final document in **SkyFire Google Drive → Release Notes**. Never overwrite the historical notes for a prior version.
2. Add the release version/date, verified user-visible highlights, caveats, the canonical full release-note link, and issue/PR references to the standing **Quarterly LinkedIn Release Notes Archive** in Drive.
3. Assign the release to its **calendar quarter** (Q1 Jan–Mar; Q2 Apr–Jun; Q3 Jul–Sep; Q4 Oct–Dec). Multiple releases in one quarter are gathered into **one optional communications event**, not separate LinkedIn posts.
4. At the owner's chosen quarterly publishing review, prepare one copy-ready LinkedIn draft from **actually released** work, preserving technical/regulatory accuracy and the user's professional voice. Ask for explicit approval before public sharing.
5. Record an item as **posted** only after the product owner confirms publication. Do not publish, schedule, email or automatically message any audience without the owner's request and authorization.

## Source and privacy guardrails
- Never represent a dated cached eCFR XML or MSHA form as a live/current filing authority without official source support. Distinguish released work from planned, beta, test-only, or incomplete work.
- Do not promote unfinished draft PRs, QA URLs or nonpublic internal mine/client documents as public release features.
- A Google Drive release-note link may be restricted. Verify external sharing permissions before including a Drive URL in a public LinkedIn post; use the public GitHub release notes or production SkyFire site when more appropriate.
- Avoid noisy routine update emails or notifications. Release records remain in GitHub/Drive; summarize at quarter review in chat.

## Relationship to CTO sprint operating agreement
This is a routine **release closeout responsibility of the CTO**, not an extra task requiring the product owner's approval after each release. Public communications remain the product owner's decision. Complete, dated release notes are mandatory whether or not a quarterly post occurs.

This policy does **not** establish autonomous background work, a recurring scheduled reminder or a LinkedIn posting integration.
