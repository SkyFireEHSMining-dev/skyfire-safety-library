THE 12 PART 50 “ACCIDENTS” — 30 CFR §50.2(h)

  1. Death of an individual at a mine.
  2. Injury to an individual at a mine with reasonable potential to cause death.
  3. Entrapment for more than 30 minutes or with reasonable potential to cause death.
  4. Unplanned inundation of a mine by a liquid or gas.
  5. Unplanned ignition or explosion of gas or dust.
  6. Unplanned fire not extinguished within 10 minutes underground, or 30 minutes at a surface mine/surface area.
  7. Unplanned ignition or explosion of a blasting agent or explosive.
  8. Qualifying unplanned roof or rib fall in active workings.
  9. Coal or rock outburst causing withdrawal of miners or disrupting regular mining activity for more than one hour.
  10. Qualifying unstable condition or failure of an impoundment, refuse pile, or culm bank.
  11. Damage to shaft/slope hoisting equipment that endangers an individual or interferes with use for more than 30 minutes.
  12. Mine event causing death or bodily injury to an individual not at the mine when the event occurs.
IF ANY OF THESE OCCUR, §50.10 IMMEDIATE NOTIFICATION APPLIES.

WHEN THE 15-MINUTE DECISION IS UNCLEAR

  • Evaluate promptly against the exact §50.2(h) definition.
  • For “reasonable potential to cause death,” use the circumstances known or reasonably knowable at the time; do not wait for a final diagnosis merely to classify the event.
  • The 15-minute period is tied to when the operator knows or should know that an accident has occurred.

KEY POINTS

  • Every Part 50 accident requires Form 7000-1.
  • Every Part 50 occupational injury or occupational illness requires Form 7000-1.
  • The emergency phone call and the 7000-1 filing are separate duties.
  • First aid by itself is not an occupational injury under Part 50; use §50.20-3 for treatment criteria.
  • The operator investigation report is separate from Form 7000-1 except for the limited small-mine exception in §50.11(b).
