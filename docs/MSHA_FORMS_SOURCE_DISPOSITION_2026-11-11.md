## Decisions and limitations
1. **No unsourced blanket currentness claim.** Verified official source/filer links are available for every shipped form, but not every cached blank is proven identical to today's MSHA-hosted PDF. The application explicitly discloses this.
2. **No blanket removal of historical blanks.** We retain local PDF references for field availability, but identify legacy or unverified versions and prioritize authoritative live sources for submission. Any later decision to remove or replace a form must preserve provenance.
3. **Source-page/content mismatch is visible.** 5000-1's current OMB package calls the form Certificate of Electrical Training while the older MSHA landing page includes Electrical/Noise; official active ICR takes priority for the current title.
4. **Source/filer QA is separate from browser/PWA QA.** Automated PDF fetch success cannot prove that interactive XFA fields work or that a form is accepted for submission. The checklist stays open until tested or clearly scoped as an offline reference.
5. **Mine rescue forms primarily concern underground operations; specialized coal forms should not imply applicability to a surface metal/nonmetal operation.** Use applicable regulations and official instructions for site-specific decisions.
